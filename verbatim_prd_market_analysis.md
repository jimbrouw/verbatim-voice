# Verbatim: Product Document & Market Analysis

## 1. Product Overview: What is Verbatim?

**Verbatim** is a minimalist, system-wide AI voice dictation application for macOS. Designed for professionals who demand speed and focus, it allows users to instantly convert their speech into text inside any active application using a seamless hotkey (Left/Right Option).

**Core Philosophy: Distraction-Free Dictation**
Unlike other modern dictation tools that clutter the screen with floating windows showing large blocks of real-time text, Verbatim employs a "micro-UI" approach. It features a tiny, unobtrusive floating pill to indicate recording state and provides just a concise, three-word snippet of real-time transcription feedback directly in the macOS Menu Bar. The text goes exactly where you want it—in your active document—without visually interrupting your workflow.

### Key Features
*   **System-Wide Integration:** Works instantly in any text field across macOS (Slack, Word, code editors, browsers).
*   **Glitch-Free Hotkey Trigger:** Uses the Left/Right Option key for rapid "push-to-talk" activation without conflicting with system shortcuts.
*   **Ultra-Minimalist UI:** 
    *   No obtrusive text expansion in the floating pill.
    *   Subtle 3-word status snippet in the Menu Bar to confirm the AI is listening and processing.
*   **Whisper-Powered Accuracy:** Built on top of robust speech-to-text models (OpenAI Whisper) to guarantee flawless transcription, even with technical jargon.
*   **Lightweight & Native:** Packaged as a clean macOS application (.dmg) that doesn't hog system resources.

## 2. Market Analysis: The Voice Dictation Landscape

To effectively market Verbatim and find a compelling name/URL, it is crucial to understand the current market dynamics, particularly what power users on platforms like Reddit are asking for.

### The Incumbent: Wispr Flow
Wispr Flow has popularized the "smart AI dictation" space on Mac. It is known for its polished UI and AI-rewriting capabilities (removing filler words, auto-formatting). 
**Why users are looking for alternatives:**
1.  **Subscription Fatigue:** Users hate paying recurring monthly fees for dictation.
2.  **Privacy Concerns:** Wispr Flow processes audio in the cloud. Many users want 100% local, on-device processing.
3.  **Resource Heavy:** It can be demanding on CPU/RAM.
4.  **Visual Distraction:** The floating UI can be too "loud" or distracting for deep-focus workers.

### Direct Competitors & Alternatives
Based on recent Reddit threads (`r/macapps`, `r/productivity`), the community frequently recommends the following alternatives:

*   **Superwhisper:** The closest direct competitor to Wispr Flow. It offers system-wide dictation but runs *locally* for privacy. It is heavily favored by power users but can be complex.
*   **MacWhisper:** Highly popular, but primarily optimized for transcribing audio/video *files* rather than instant system-wide dictation (though it has dictation features).
*   **Voibe:** Gaining traction for being extremely lightweight and offering a "real-time" feel without the lag or heavy resource footprint of Wispr Flow.
*   **Sotto:** Praised for its excellent "push-to-talk" workflow and minimal latency.

### What the Market is Begging For (The "Reddit Consensus")
When Redditors ask for Wispr Flow alternatives, their core demands are:
1.  **"I want it to be local/private."** (No audio sent to the cloud).
2.  **"I want a one-time purchase or free version."** (Anti-subscription).
3.  **"I want it to be fast and out of my way."** (Low latency, low CPU usage).

## 3. Product Positioning & Naming Strategy

### Where Verbatim Wins
Verbatim wins on **Focus and Minimalism**. While Wispr Flow and Superwhisper try to be "AI writing assistants" that rewrite your thoughts, Verbatim is a pure, frictionless conduit between your brain and the screen. 

**The Hook:** *The dictation app that stays out of your way.*

### Brainstorming Angles for Naming & URLs

When searching for a domain, focus on keywords that convey speed, minimalism, clarity, and voice. Since "Verbatim" is a strong word, you might face domain availability issues. Here are thematic angles for alternative names or domain prefixes/suffixes:

**Theme 1: Purity & Accuracy (Focus on "Verbatim" nature)**
*   *Keywords:* True, Exact, Clear, Pure, Scribe, Type.
*   *Name Ideas:* VerbatimApp, TrueType, PureDictate, ScribeMac, ExactlyVoice.
*   *URL Ideas:* getverbatim.com, useverbatim.app, verbatimvoice.com, verbatim-mac.com.

**Theme 2: Speed & Flow (Focus on the push-to-talk latency)**
*   *Keywords:* Flow, Swift, Dash, Snap, Flash.
*   *Name Ideas:* SwiftSpeak, SnapType, FlowDictate, VoiceDash.
*   *URL Ideas:* swiftspeak.app, dashvoice.com.

**Theme 3: Minimalism & Focus (Focus on the hidden UI)**
*   *Keywords:* Stealth, Micro, Zen, Ghost, Blank.
*   *Name Ideas:* ZenVoice, StealthDictate, MicroScribe, GhostType.
*   *URL Ideas:* zendictate.com, stealthvoice.app.

### Next Steps for You (The Creator)
1.  **Decide on the Business Model:** Will Verbatim be a one-time purchase ($19-$29 is the sweet spot on Reddit) or Open Source / Freemium?
2.  **Confirm Privacy Posture:** Does Verbatim run Whisper locally (via `whisper.cpp`) or via API? If local, make **PRIVACY** your #1 marketing headline.
3.  **Domain Hunt:** Go to Namecheap or Cloudflare and check `.app`, `.io`, or `get[name].com` variations of your favorite names.
4.  **Reddit Launch Strategy:** Once you have a landing page, post in `r/macapps` with the title: *"I loved Wispr Flow but hated the distracting UI and subscriptions. So I built [Name]—a minimalist, system-wide dictation app that stays out of your way."* This exact framing performs exceptionally well.
